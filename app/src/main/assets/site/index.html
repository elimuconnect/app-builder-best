At Elimu Connect Ltd, we are on a mission to transform education in Kenya and beyond by making quality learning accessible, affordable, and impactful—especially for students in underserved communities. We are a registered private limited company committed to empowering learners and educators through innovative solutions in academic coaching, educational publishing, ICT integration, school supplies, and teacher recruitment. Our unique approach blends technology, community engagement, and social impact, ensuring that no child is left behind due to financial or geographic barriers. Whether it’s a student improving in STEM and languages, a teacher getting connected to a job, or a school receiving vital educational tools, Elimu Connect is the bridge to opportunity.

Our Mission
